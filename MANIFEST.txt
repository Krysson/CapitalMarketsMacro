   tolerant field/name matching), debt_to_penny(),
   interest_expense_fytd(). data.net_liquidity() now prefers the
   daily DTS TGA over FRED's weekly WTREGEN, falling back per house
   rules. Fed page gains a three-metric fiscal tape panel.

   AND Treasury; the red BLS FEED DOWN line is suppressed only when
   Akamai blocks BLS and the Google backup answers (any other dead
   feed still shows); 64 direct use_container_width kwargs swept to
   width='stretch' across all pages (theme.plot translation from
   v4.8.1 remains as the backstop).
4  OPEX CALENDAR — desk/events.opex_dates(): third Fridays +
   quarterly triple-witching flags, pure computation, nothing to
   fail. Rendered as chips on the Calendar page.
5  VARIANCE RISK PREMIUM — Volatility page panel: VIX vs 21-day
   realized SPY vol, sign-colored readout, methodology-gap note
   (teaches the sign, not the basis). [T1 implied · T2 realized]
6  VOL REGIME TAG — composite CALM/ELEVATED/STRESS/CRISIS chip from
   VIX level + VIX/VIX3M, on data already fetched by the page.
7  EARNINGS IV — Quote page ERN expander: front-expiry ATM IV +
   straddle-implied move, app-side chain call, cached 15 min,
   fail-soft with named reasons. [T2 chain-derived]
8  FLOW BY EXPIRY — Flow page display cut of the existing
   footprint record grouped by expiration date; no new feed.
9  THE WALLS FIX (live bug, 06-Aug) — Yahoo returned a chain with
   openInterest zeroed on every row while volume populated; the
   accrual stored it and the walls rendered "PUT WALL (0 OI)".
   Three-layer fix: desk/accrue.py refuses to overwrite a good
   record with a zero-OI snapshot (named message names the kept
   date); the Volatility page withholds walls on a degraded record
   with a dated red explanation; desk/analyst.py hands the Analyst
   "[T1 gap]" instead of fictional walls.
