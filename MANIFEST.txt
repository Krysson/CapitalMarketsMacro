README.md  ::  # Capital Markets Desk (Streamlit)

PAPER DESK (PAPER / PB): homegrown simulator covering every asset
class the desk watches — equities/ETFs, futures at REAL contract
multipliers (ES $50/pt, CL 1000bbl, GC 100oz, 14 specs), FX, crypto;
options deferred to 4.1 and said so on-page. Fills = desk mark +
slippage ALWAYS against you (1 tick futures, 2-10bps by class),
slippage tracked cumulatively. THE TICKET ENFORCES THE FUNNEL:
refuses orders without a named generator, all five gates, and a
kill-switch sentence; survivable-size check vs cash. Open positions
show the kill switch beside P&L; closes prompt the reckoning; closed
ledger + P&L-by-generator table. Book = local JSON, export/restore,
two-press reset. LIVE TOGGLE beside the command bar on every page:
market_history/ohlc/ticker_snapshot on ~60s cache (headless bot
always slow-path). theme.VERSION 4.0.0. No new keys, no new hosting.
