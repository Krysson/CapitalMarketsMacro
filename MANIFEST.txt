FILES
  desk/flow.py       — normalize_flows(): latest session per fund as
                       % of prior-day AUM, 5-session rolling sum, and
                       a z-score vs the fund's OWN accrued record
                       (honest small-n rule: no z until 10 sessions,
                       n printed). shortvol_history() +
                       shortvol_percentiles(): today's short ratio as
                       a percentile of each symbol's own record.
  desk/accrue.py     — accrues history/shortvol.csv (date, symbol,
                       short_ratio) one session per day, deduped by
                       file date, reusing finra_short()'s cache —
                       the accrue-your-own pattern from flows/
                       signals, because FINRA has no history
                       endpoint. Includes v4.9.0's zero-OI guard.
  pages/17_Flow.py   — FLOW SCOREBOARD strip (4 chips: loudest ETF
                       flow vs itself, highest short ratio + own-
                       record percentile, heaviest dark-pool name,
                       footprint intensity percentile — all from
                       calls the page already caches; each fails
                       soft to an em-dash with a named reason).
                       Measurement-pass table in the ETF section
                       (%AUM / 5-day / z / n). Short-vol section
                       gains a VS OWN RECORD readout with honest
                       record-building notes until n=10. ATS table
                       gains a vs-4-week-average column. Footprints
                       gain Notional $mm, % from spot, and a CAMP
                       flag (same contract on 2+ of last 5 record
                       days).
  desk/theme.py      — VERSION 4.9.1.

NOTES

  short-ratio record starts accruing with the first app load after
  deploy, and z-scores on ETF flows arrive when the flow record
  (started 23-Jul) reaches 10 sessions per fund. Expect "record
  n=X" subtitles for the first stretch; that is the feature working,
  not failing. FINRA short INTEREST (the settled twice-monthly
  stock figure) is deliberately NOT in this release — parked to
