RSP is the equal-weight S&P; SPY is cap-weighted. When RSP outperforms SPY (RSP/SPY ratio rising), the average stock is participating — this is broad strength, not a megacap-driven illusion. +0.82% is constructive but modest. CIRCUIT 4 — CROSS-ASSET
HYG/LQD credit spread: +1.79%/1m. [F]

HYG = high-yield bond ETF; LQD = investment-grade bond ETF. When HYG outperforms LQD, credit markets are risk-on — investors are comfortable reaching down the quality spectrum. HY OAS (option-adjusted spread — the extra yield high-yield bonds pay over Treasuries, stripped of embedded options) at 268 bps [F] is historically tight. IG OAS at 78 bps [F] is very tight. Credit is pricing near-perfection. Tight OAS is a risk-on confirmation but also means there's limited cushion if the narrative cracks. [I]

DXY -0.2%/1m [F] — dollar softness is marginally supportive for commodities and EM. Copper +7.0%/1m [F] echoes the growth signal. Gold at $4,056 [F] is large — that's not just inflation hedging, it's a reserve/geopolitical bid layered on top. [I] Don't conflate Gold's signal with the equity risk-on read.

CIRCUIT 5 — RATES & CREDIT
2s10s +0.36pp. 3m10y +0.78pp. 10Y at 4.67%. [F]

Both spreads are positive — the curve is upward-sloping (steepening; longer yields exceed shorter yields). This is the normal shape and historically consistent with expansion. The 3m10y at +78bps is particularly meaningful because it had been inverted (a recession signal) and is now clearly in positive territory. Re-steepening via the long end rising is the risk: if the 10Y continues climbing on inflation repricing, duration (interest-rate sensitivity of bond prices) gets punished.

The 10Y at 4.67% is the load-bearing tension point. [F] The Fed is easing, breakevens are rising, and the long end is pricing accordingly. If FOMC next week signals a pause or hawkish tilt, the 10Y moves higher and the equity multiple (P/E is inversely sensitive to the discount rate) compresses. [I]
